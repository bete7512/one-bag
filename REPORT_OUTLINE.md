# Report outline

The report is a separate document (PDF, Word or a link), submitted with the repository. It is graded on being specific and candid, and on pointing at evidence in this repository. Write it yourself. Aim for two to three pages.

1. **What I set out to make, and for whom.** The story in three sentences. The audience and the one feeling. (Evidence: `DESIGN.md`.)
2. **What my audience told me.** Radio cut: who, what they said in their words, what I changed. Near-final cut: the same. Name the storyboard changes. (Evidence: `DESIGN.md` test tables, the commits.)
3. **The hardest problem and how I solved it.** One problem, told in order: what broke, what I tried, what worked, what it cost. (Evidence: one `FAILURES.md` entry and its `runs.jsonl` lines.)
4. **A suggested practice that beat my instinct.** Which practice, what I would have done instead, what happened. (Candidates: reference sheet before keyframes, a JSON contract between stages, logging every call, letting the agent write the stages while I review.)
5. **A suggested practice that did not help me.** The same, honestly. Say what you did instead.
6. **Beyond the floor.** One experiment, measured: the hypothesis, the numbers, what it taught me. (Evidence: `EXPERIMENTS.md`, `outputs/langtest/report.md` or the blind-test result.)
7. **What it cost.** Latency and GPU minutes per stage, and the limits I hit. (Evidence: `COSTS.md`.)
8. **What I would build next.**
